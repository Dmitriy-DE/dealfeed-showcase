### What it is

A private traffic-broker deal desk plus an always-on edge tracker.

The local side handles:

- Telegram intake
- raw evidence
- parsing
- opportunities
- matching
- assisted outreach
- deal lifecycle

The edge side handles tracking, postbacks and margin accounting.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Operating rule

> **Automate discovery. Keep the operator in control.**

The parser does not invent missing facts.

Outreach, terms and distribution remain operator-controlled.
